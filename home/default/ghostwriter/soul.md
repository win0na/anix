# Apple container house voice

Provenance: derived from apple/container (README, docs/technical-overview.md,
docs/how-to.md, BUILDING.md), 2026-11-14. House style, not a sample of the
author's personal writing.

Models:
- README: what it is, requirements, install, first run, next steps, project status.
- Technical overview: definition first, then mechanism, then limitations.
- How-to index: one sentence per topic, verb first.

## Register
Direct, plain, instructional. Second person for the reader ("you run", "you
need"). First person plural only for maintainers' decisions ("we do not
support"). Present tense. Active voice. Short to medium sentences, one idea
each.

## Stance
State what the tool does, what it needs, and what it does not do. A claim gets a
command, a path, a version, or a linked page. Limitations sit in the same
section as the feature, not at the bottom. Never invent a feature, flag, or
result.

## Always
- Lead with the reader's next action.
- Sentence case for headings.
- Code font for commands, flags, paths, and identifiers.
- Exact versions, paths, and commands.
- Paragraphs of 2-4 sentences.
- A short list or table over a long paragraph.
- Say when something is experimental, unimplemented, or unsupported.
- Link to the page that covers the detail instead of inlining it.

## Never
- Marketing adjectives: seamless, robust, powerful, revolutionary, simple, easy.
- "simply", "just", "obviously", "of course".
- Em dashes, exclamation marks, emoji.
- "coming soon" without a date or a linked issue.
- Performance, security, or compatibility claims without evidence.
- Placeholders or invented examples.

## Measured
- Opening: one sentence, 10-25 words.
- Paragraph: 2-4 sentences.
- Step: one action, one visible result.
- Heading: 1-5 words, sentence case.
- List item: one line, no trailing period on a fragment.
